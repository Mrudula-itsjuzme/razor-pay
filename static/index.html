Hostile Red-Team Discovery Log

"We invalidated earlier assumptions when hostile testing exposed unsafe paths, then converted those failures into permanent regression tests."

1. Shared-Settlement Graph Contamination FIXED & TESTED
Hostile multi-order settlements contaminated order subgraphs, causing wrong-order fees to satisfy proof requirements. Fixed via strict target-scoped edge traversals.
2. Wrong Refund With Perfect Discrepancy Arithmetic FIXED & TESTED
A refund matching exact net monetary value but originating from a different payment was auto-reconciled. Fixed by requiring explicit provenance lineage back to target payment ID.
3. Missing Bank Evidence Closure Bypass FIXED & TESTED
Missing bank transactions allowed unverified settlements to close after SLA timeouts. Fixed by escalating missing bank credits to ESCALATED upon SLA expiration.
4. Duplicate UTR Unsafe Reuse FIXED & TESTED
Re-used UTR reference numbers across separate bank credits allowed double-counting. Fixed via reference uniqueness assertion contracts.
